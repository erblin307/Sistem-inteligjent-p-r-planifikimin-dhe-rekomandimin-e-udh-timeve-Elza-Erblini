# Presentation review findings

Issues found in the code while preparing the presentation (2026-10-08, commit `66a00b8`). **None of them were fixed in this change.** It is documentation only. They are recorded so they can be planned separately, and so the presenters know about them before the jury does.

Ordered by how likely they are to come up in the defense.

| # | Finding | Where | Effect | Suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The recommendation engine is not called anywhere. No route, page or service imports it. | `src/server/engines/recommendation` | The "intelligent" part of the system cannot be seen in the app. | Call `buildRecommendations` from the Hotels and Activities pages (or a `/trips/:id/recommendations/*` endpoint) with catalog rows mapped to candidates. |
| 2 | The workspace scores contradict the engine. The Hotels tab shows Casa Fuster 92 and Praktik Bakery 86 with the reason "Within your nightly budget". For the same €1,500 Balanced 2-person trip, the engine gives Casa Fuster 78 and Praktik Bakery 88, because €142 is above the €120 nightly allowance. | `src/lib/fixtures/barcelona.ts` | A jury member comparing slide 9 with the demo sees two different rankings. | Fixed by #1. Until then, label the scores as sample values. |
| 3 | The recommendation engine and the form validation have no automated tests. | `src/server/engines/recommendation`, `src/contracts/trip-brief.ts` | The core logic is unprotected against regressions. | Unit tests per component, plus golden tests for a few fixed briefs (the engine is deterministic). |
| 4 | Any trip id shows the Barcelona trip. `/trips/rome/budget` answers 200 with Barcelona data. | `src/app/(app)/trips/[tripId]/layout.tsx` and pages | Unknown trip ids are not handled. | Call `notFound()` for ids that do not exist once trips are loaded from the database. |
| 5 | `POST /api/v1/trips/generate` does not use the shared `route()` wrapper: no same-origin check, no `x-request-id`, unexpected errors are not logged, and the `Idempotency-Key` the form sends is ignored. | `src/app/api/v1/trips/generate/route.ts` | Inconsistent with the other endpoints. Low risk today because it writes nothing. | Wrap it in `route()` from `src/server/platform/http.ts`. |
| 6 | Two different trip-brief contracts. The form uses `destination` (text), `children` (a count), `public-transit`, and no pace. The database API uses `destinationId`, `childAges`, `public_transport`, and `pace`. Diet lists also differ. | `src/contracts/trip-brief.ts`, `src/contracts/trip.ts` | The form's brief cannot be saved through the trips API without a mapping. The interest slug `food-markets` in the form is `food` in the development catalog. | Pick one contract, or add an explicit mapper with tests. |
| 7 | Interest match divides by the number of selected interests. With two interests, a perfect single-topic place scores 50. With five, it scores 20. | `tagMatch` in `engine.ts` | Choosing more interests lowers every single-topic place, which is counter-intuitive. | Consider the best match, or a smoothed share. A design decision, not a bug. |
| 8 | The budget penalty is steep: 2 points per 1% over the allowance. A €35 activity against a €22.50 daily allowance scores 0 for budget. The allowance is per day, compared with the price of one activity. | `budgetMatch`, `scoreActivity` in `engine.ts` | Popular paid sights are pushed down hard for Balanced budgets. | Revisit the slope, or compare against a per-activity share. A design decision, not a bug. |
| 9 | Interactive-looking controls do nothing: the day buttons and arrows on the Itinerary tab, and "Select hotel" on the Hotels tab. | `itinerary/page.tsx`, `hotel-card.tsx` | Can confuse a live demo. | Wire them up, or disable them until they work. |
| 10 | `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` describes a broader design (TanStack Query, React Hook Form, MapLibre, Bayesian rating, MMR diversity, itinerary and budget engines) without marking which parts exist. | `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` | A reviewer reading it may assume these are built. | Add an implementation-status column, or a note at the top pointing to `PRESENTATION_PLAN.md` §1. |
